Important: GARMI GUARD is an educational and preparedness product. It is not a medical service, diagnostic tool, emergency service or substitute for professional medical care.

1. General educational information

Content about hydration, cooling, food routines, sleep routines, heat-risk awareness and emergency readiness is general information. Individual needs vary according to health conditions, age, pregnancy, medications, occupation, local weather, housing and other factors.

2. Medical emergencies

Severe heat-related symptoms can be dangerous. Do not rely on the product or website instead of seeking urgent professional help. If a person is confused, collapses, has seizures, has severe worsening symptoms or appears to be experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or qualified medical professionals immediately.

3. No guaranteed outcome

GARMI GUARD does not guarantee a specific indoor temperature, health result, electricity saving, sleep improvement or prevention of heat illness. Outcomes depend on environment, building conditions, equipment, personal circumstances and correct use of relevant actions.

4. Local conditions and professional advice

Users should follow official weather alerts, local public-health guidance, workplace/school requirements and advice from qualified professionals. People with medical conditions or special risks should seek individualized medical guidance.

5. External services

The website may use third-party hosting, forms or payment services. Their availability and policies are outside GARMI GUARD’s direct control.

6. Contact

For product-related questions, use the Contact Us page. Do not use the contact form as an emergency channel.
