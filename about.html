About Us

What GARMI GUARD is: a digital interactive Summer Survival System. It is designed to organize practical summer-preparedness actions into a clear, usable structure.

Our purpose

Extreme summer conditions create many small, connected problems: hydration is forgotten, rooms trap heat, sleep becomes difficult, routines break down and people may not know the warning signs that require urgent attention. GARMI GUARD brings these topics into one structured digital experience.

What the system covers

Daily systems

Hydration planning, food routine guidance, morning-to-night summer planning and trackers.

Cooling & sleep

Room cooling guidance, airflow and shade checks, and practical hot-night sleep routines.

Preparedness

Heat-risk awareness, emergency-readiness guidance and situation-specific plans for students, PG/hostel residents, families and outdoor routines.

How we communicate

We aim to describe the product accurately. GARMI GUARD is not positioned as medical treatment, a guaranteed cooling device, or a replacement for air-conditioning, medical care or emergency services. It is an educational planning system that helps users organize practical actions.

Digital-first delivery

GARMI GUARD is delivered digitally. There is no physical shipment. Product pricing, delivery conditions, refund eligibility and privacy practices are published on this website before purchase.
