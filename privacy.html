Privacy Policy

How GARMI GUARD collects, uses, stores and protects information submitted through the website and digital product access flow.

Home / Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you visit the GARMI GUARD website, submit a lead form, contact support or purchase the digital product once payments are enabled.

1. Information we collect

  • Lead form information such as name and phone number.
  • Contact form information such as name, phone number, email address and message.
  • Technical and attribution information such as page URL, referrer URL, UTM source/medium/campaign, device type, theme preference and user-agent string.
  • Transaction references and payment status when a payment provider is integrated. We do not intend to store card PINs, CVV, UPI PINs or banking passwords.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • To deliver or restore digital access after confirmed payment.
  • To manage eligible promotional access and product communications.
  • To maintain records, prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To understand campaign source and improve the website.
  • To comply with legal, accounting or dispute-resolution obligations.

3. Storage and service providers

Lead and contact submissions may be stored in separate Google Sheets through Google Apps Script web applications configured by the site operator. Payment information, when payments are enabled, is processed by the payment service shown at checkout and may be subject to that provider’s privacy practices.

4. Local storage

The website may use browser local storage to remember theme preferences, lead-submission status, product-flow state and promotional timing. Clearing browser storage may reset these local preferences.

5. Sharing of personal information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate forms, hosting, payment processing, access delivery or legal compliance. We may disclose information where required by law or a valid legal process.

6. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for support, access delivery, record keeping, fraud prevention, disputes and legal compliance. Retention periods can vary depending on the purpose and applicable requirements.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce unauthorized access or misuse. No internet-based system is completely risk-free, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification and legal or record-keeping obligations. You can also clear local browser storage through your browser settings.

9. Children

The website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from young children without appropriate involvement of a parent or lawful guardian where required.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when services, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be submitted through the Contact Us page.
