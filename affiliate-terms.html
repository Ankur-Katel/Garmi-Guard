Affiliate Program Terms

These terms explain how the GARMI GUARD affiliate program works. The product is a digital interactive Summer Survival System, not a physical product.

1. Commission

  • Affiliate commission is 30% per eligible sale attributed to your referral link.
  • Commission applies only after payment is verified and the sale is not cancelled, duplicated or refunded.
  • Commission is calculated on the actual selling price collected for that order, excluding failed/duplicate payments.

2. Minimum payout

  • Minimum payout threshold: ₹100.
  • Payout is made to the UPI ID submitted during affiliate registration.
  • Payout timing may vary depending on manual order verification, fraud checks and payment confirmation.

3. Referral attribution

  • Your referral link contains an encoded phone reference in the URL as ?ref=CODE.
  • The payment sheet records the encoded ref and decoded affiliate phone for attribution.
  • Referral attribution may not work if the user deletes browser data, changes devices or removes the referral parameter before checkout.

4. Promotion rules

  • Do not make fake income, fake medical, fake refund, fake government or fake guarantee claims.
  • Do not spam WhatsApp groups, comment sections, inboxes or communities.
  • Do not use misleading screenshots, fake reviews or fake purchase notifications in your promotion.
  • Use clear language: GARMI GUARD is a digital interactive summer guidance system.

5. Approval and removal

  • GARMI GUARD may pause or remove affiliate participation if promotion is misleading, abusive, fraudulent or harmful to customers.
  • Incorrect UPI ID, invalid phone number or suspicious transactions may delay payout.

6. Contact

For affiliate questions, use the Contact Us page and include your registered phone number and UPI ID.
